Advanced Blanket Sales Agreement

Agree long-term quantities and prices with a customer, take the agreement through a sequential multi-tier approval, and issue it as a formal PDF by e-mail. Call-off quotations, quantity consumption, expiry reminders and the final expiration notice then run on their own.

Why this module

Odoo 18 has blanket orders on the purchase side only. On the sales side, framework agreements usually live in spreadsheets and e-mail threads, away from the quotations they govern.

In standard Odoo 18

  • No sales blanket agreement document: agreed volumes and prices are tracked outside Odoo.
  • No approval cycle for a customer commitment before it is sent.
  • No delivery schedule tied to a commercial agreement.
  • Legal terms are free text on each quotation, with no governing law or termination notice fields.
  • Nothing stops a salesperson from ordering more than was agreed.
  • No automatic reminder before expiry, so renewals are missed.

With this module

  • A Blanket Agreements document under Sales with lines, prices, discounts, taxes and live Ordered / Remaining quantities.
  • Approval templates with sequential tiers; every approver gets a mail.activity task.
  • A Delivery Schedules tab, with one-click quotations per scheduled delivery.
  • A Legal Terms & Conditions tab: governing law, dispute resolution, termination notice and rich-text terms.
  • Confirmation of a linked sales order checks the remaining quantity and the agreement validity.
  • A daily scheduled action e-mails the pre-expiry reminder and the final expiration notice.

How it works

Four steps from draft to a running agreement.

1
Draft
The salesperson enters lines, delivery schedule and legal terms. They become the Responsible user.
2
Approve
Submit for Approval opens tier 1. Each approval closes the tier’s activities and opens the next tier.
3
Issue
The Responsible user gets an Issue Agreement task. Issuing e-mails the formal PDF to the customer.
4
Run & expire
Quotations draw down the agreed quantities. The daily job sends the reminder and the expiration notice, then expires the agreement.

Features

The screenshots use a demo company, Al Noor Industrial Supplies (Jeddah, SAR), with nine agreements across every status. Sara is the salesperson. Khalid and Nora are Finance Controllers, and Omar is the Sales Director.

Menus & settings

Off by default, one switch to turn on

Enable it in Sales › Configuration › Settings › Blanket Sales Agreements. The same section sets the company’s default approval template, the default reminder days before expiry and the default terms & conditions, and links to Approval Templates and Approval Roles. Once enabled, the menus appear: Sales › Orders › Blanket Agreements and Sales › Configuration › Blanket Agreements.

Feature 1

Every agreement, its status and its next action in one list

The list shows the customer, the Responsible user, the validity dates, the agreement total and a Consumed (%) bar. The Activities column shows the pending approval or issue task. Agreements close to their end date are highlighted. Filters include My Agreements, My Pending Approvals, each status and Expiring within 30 Days. You can group by customer, responsible, status or end month.

Feature 2

Order lines with live consumption

Each line holds the Agreed Qty, Unit of Measure, Unit Price, Disc.% and Taxes. The price is suggested from the pricelist (or the product price) when the product is chosen, then stays as negotiated: changing the quantity or the pricelist never overwrites it. Taxes are mapped through the customer’s fiscal position. Ordered Qty, Delivered Qty and Remaining Qty are computed from confirmed sales orders, with UoM conversion. The footer adds Ordered Amount and Consumed (%) to the usual totals.

BSA/2026/0001: 120 t of rebar already ordered, 360 t remaining. The chatter shows the formal e-mail sent to the customer with the agreement PDF.
Feature 3

Delivery schedules that turn into quotations

Plan deliveries per agreement line with a Scheduled Date, Quantity and Delivery Notes. Once the agreement is issued, the button on a schedule row creates a quotation for that quantity, with the scheduled date as the delivery date. The row then shows Planned, Quoted, Ordered or Delivered. Scheduled dates must fall within the agreement period, and scheduled quantities cannot exceed the agreed quantity.

Feature 4

Legal terms & conditions on the agreement itself

The Legal Terms & Conditions tab holds Governing Law, Dispute Resolution, Termination Notice (Days) and a rich-text Terms & Conditions body. The body is pre-filled from the company default and can be edited per agreement. All of it is printed on the PDF.

Feature 5

Approval templates with roles and sequential tiers

An Approval Role groups the users allowed to approve, such as Finance Controller or Sales Director. An Approval Template lists the tiers in order. Each tier is assigned either to a Role (any member can approve) or to a Specific User. Use different templates for different policies; for example, a 3-tier Key Account template for large agreements.

Feature 6

Approvers work from their activities, even without Sales rights

Submit for Approval schedules an Agreement Approval activity for every approver of the first tier, with the customer and total in the note. A banner shows who the agreement is waiting for. Only the current tier’s approvers see Approve and Reject. The approver does not need any Sales access rights: Khalid below is a finance user who can only open agreements he has to approve.

When a tier is approved, the approver’s activity is marked done, the other approvers of the same tier have theirs removed, and the next tier opens. The Approvals tab keeps the trail: submission, tier, role, approvers, decision, who decided and when.

With Agreement Segregation of Duties (on by default), the Responsible user never approves their own agreement, and one person approves at most one tier of a submission. Submission is refused when a template cannot be approved by a different person at each tier. Approving is refused when it would leave a later tier with nobody to approve it. The approval rows themselves are written by the workflow only, so not even a Sales Administrator can add an approver to an open tier.

Feature 7

Rejection with a reason, then resubmit

Reject asks for a Rejection Reason, records it on the tier, removes the open approval activities and notifies the Responsible user. The agreement lines become editable again. Resubmit starts a new submission; the earlier decisions, including the rejection reason, stay in the Approvals tab as history. Reset to Draft is also available.

Feature 8

Issue it: the formal PDF goes to the customer

When the last tier signs off, the agreement is Approved. The Responsible user receives an Issue Agreement activity and a notification. Issue Agreement (allowed for the Responsible user or a Sales Administrator) sets the issue date, closes the activity and e-mails the customer in their own language with the agreement PDF attached. If the customer has no e-mail address, the agreement is still issued and a note is logged.

Feature 9

Expiry reminder and final notice, automatically

Each agreement has its own Reminder Before Expiry (Days); the computed reminder date is shown next to it. The daily scheduled action Blanket Agreements: Expiry Reminders & Notices does three things. It e-mails the reminder once that date is reached. On the end date, it sends the final expiration notice. The next day, it sets the agreement to Expired. Each e-mail is sent once only.

Feature 10

Quotations from the agreement, with quantity control

Create Quotation on an issued agreement creates a quotation for what is neither ordered nor already in an open quotation. The quotation copies the agreed price, discount and taxes, and links each line to its agreement line. It is always in the agreement’s currency: it uses the agreement’s pricelist, or another pricelist in that currency, so the agreed prices are never converted. Lines added by hand with an Agreement Line get the agreed price and discount too. Sales orders carry a Blanket Agreement field and the Sales Orders smart button lists them. Confirmation is refused when:

  • the agreement is not issued;
  • today is outside its validity period;
  • the customer does not match the agreement;
  • the order is in another currency;
  • a linked line is not at the agreed net price (a Sales Administrator may confirm anyway; the difference is logged on the order);
  • or the quantity exceeds what remains.
Rule: remaining quantity = agreed quantity − quantities on confirmed sales orders, converted to the agreement line’s unit. Example: 320 t agreed and nothing confirmed yet, so a quotation for 400 t is blocked at confirmation. Several orders confirmed together are checked as one total, and the same quantity and price checks run again whenever a line of a confirmed order is changed or added.
Feature 11

A modern PDF, in English and in right-to-left Arabic

The Blanket Sales Agreement report prints in the customer’s language. It includes the parties, agreement details, agreed products and prices, totals, the delivery schedule, the legal terms, the internal approval trail and signature blocks. A dedicated paper format leaves room for the company header, so the table never overlaps it, including the repeated table header on later pages. In Arabic, the columns, alignment and totals mirror to right-to-left.

Feature 12

Fully translated to Arabic

Every label, button, status, message, activity and error is translated to Arabic (ar_001). The three customer e-mails have complete right-to-left Arabic versions, and Arabic-speaking customers receive them automatically.

Agreement statuses

What each status means and what can happen next.

Draft everything is editable; Submit for Approval or Cancel
Waiting Approval content locked on the server; one tier at a time, current approvers can Approve or Reject
Approved all tiers signed off; only the end date and delivery schedule can change; the Responsible user can Issue it
Issued sent to the customer; quotations can be created and confirmed against it; only the end date and delivery schedule can change
Expired set by the daily job the day after the end date; no new orders can be confirmed
Rejected reason recorded; edit and Resubmit, or Reset to Draft
Cancelled cancelling is refused once confirmed sales orders exist; Reset to Draft to reuse
Timeline example: end date 31/12, reminder 30 days. On 01/12 the customer receives the reminder. On 31/12 (still valid) they receive the expiration notice. On 01/01 the agreement becomes Expired. If no template is set, submitting approves the agreement immediately.

Built for real-world corner cases

Behaviour that is covered in the code and, where marked, by the automated tests.

Approver outside the sales team
Approvers only need to be internal users. They can open, approve, reject and comment on the agreements assigned to them, and see nothing else.
Wrong person clicks Approve
Only approvers of the current tier can decide, never the Responsible user, and nobody twice in one submission. Tested.
Empty role in a tier
Submission is refused when a tier has no active approver, so an agreement can never get stuck waiting for nobody. Tested.
Different units of measure
Ordered and remaining quantities convert sales order units to the agreement line unit. The line unit must be in the product’s UoM category.
Orders changed after confirmation
Raising a quantity, changing a price or adding a linked line on a confirmed order is checked against the agreement again. Tested.
Customer without e-mail
Issuing still works and a note in the chatter records that no e-mail could be sent (tested). The cron logs the same note for reminders and notices.
Cron runs every day
Reminders and notices are flagged once sent and never repeat (tested). Each agreement is processed in its own savepoint, so one failure does not block the others.
Multi-currency & pricelists
Agreements created by import or API take the customer’s pricelist like the form. Quotations stay in the agreement currency, and orders in another currency cannot be confirmed against it. Tested.
Deleting and cancelling
Only draft, rejected or cancelled agreements can be deleted. Cancelling is refused once confirmed sales orders exist. Agreement lines used on orders and schedules on confirmed orders cannot be deleted. Tested.
Edits after submission
Lines, prices, dates, customer and legal terms are locked on the server, not only on screen, once the agreement is submitted. Issued agreements still accept a later end date. Tested.
Open quotations
Create Quotation skips quantities already in open quotations, and each product appears once per agreement, so every order line maps to exactly one agreement line. Tested.
Past end date
An agreement whose end date has passed cannot be submitted or issued until the end date is extended. Delivery schedules must use lines of their own agreement. Tested.

Security

Uses the standard Sales groups; enabling the feature grants read-only approver access to internal users.

WhoSees and does
Internal user (feature enabled)Read-only: agreements they are responsible for or are an approver on; approve, reject and comment when they are the current approver
Sales / User: Own Documents OnlyCreates and manages their own agreements, submits, issues as Responsible, creates quotations
Sales / User: All DocumentsThe same on all agreements of their companies
Sales / AdministratorEverything above, plus Approval Templates and Approval Roles; can issue any approved agreement and confirm an order at a price that differs from the agreement (logged on the order)
Approval trailRead-only for everyone, administrators included; only the approval workflow writes it
Lines, schedules, approval rowsVisible only to users who can see the agreement (own documents, all documents or approver)
Multi-companyAgreements, lines, delivery schedules, approval rows, templates and roles are restricted to the user’s allowed companies; templates and roles can also be shared across companies
